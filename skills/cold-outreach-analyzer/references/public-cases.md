# Public First-Person Cases

These cases are illustrative self-reports, not verified records or representative samples. They test whether the framework preserves mixed outcomes and does not infer rates or causal effects. Only the minimal information needed to interpret the signal is summarized. No private applicant correspondence is included.

## How to use the cases

- Use them as counterexamples to overconfident readings: interviews, strong encouragement, and funding conversations can be followed by rejection; successful admission can occur without prior outreach.
- Do not estimate percentages, likelihood ratios, or tier-to-admit mappings from them.
- Do not assume an unanswered email and no email are equivalent.
- Preserve each case's degree, field, application cycle, outcome source, and missing information.
