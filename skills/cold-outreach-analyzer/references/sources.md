## Design conclusions drawn from the sources

- A reply's meaning depends on the process. Advance advisor contact is optional in some programs and essential in others.
- Institutional authority, supervisor intent, recruitment capacity, and funding must be recorded separately.
- Silence can reflect volume, timing, or policy; a faculty member may be recruiting and still unable to answer every email.
- An enthusiastic supervisor can help but cannot guarantee a program offer.
- A supervisor's inability to recruit constrains that supervisor/position/cycle unless the official program decision says more.
- These sources support a contextual analysis workflow, not a calibrated ranking or numerical forecast.
